# Ethical interaction patterns
**Use when:** subscriptions, retention, cancellation, consent, paywalls, upsells, confirm dialogs.

- Do not use guilt/shame copy, disguised ads, false scarcity, prechecked consent, misleading hierarchy, or difficult-to-find opt-out paths.
- Cancellation/unsubscribe/delete flows should explain consequences neutrally and remain straightforward. An optional reversal/rejoin action can be offered *after* completion, without obstructing departure.
- Keep destructive and safe actions clearly labeled; do not turn color or size into deceptive steering.
- Show accurate progress and availability; don't fake urgency or status messages. Confirm genuine completion before announcing it.
- Treat onboarding and progress nudges as supportive, not coercive.

**QA:** can users reasonably decline, exit, recover, and understand the consequence? Does visual treatment hide a valid choice?
