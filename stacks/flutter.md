# Flutter
**Use when:** editing Flutter interfaces.
- Use ThemeData/ColorScheme and shared widgets; follow Material/Cupertino conventions for the product platform.
- Support textScaleFactor and semantic labels; keep hit areas comfortable and gesture alternatives available.
- Prefer constraint-aware layouts; test small displays, keyboard overlays, safe areas and dynamic content.
- Keep animation work efficient and respect platform reduce-motion settings when accessible.
- Avoid arbitrary web-style CSS spacing or widgets that ignore native scrolling/back navigation.
